# RetroPick Board Memo

## 1) Executive thesis

RetroPick should win by becoming the default deterministic event-contract layer for recurring market expression, not by trying to be a full exchange too early.  

The core advantage is credible and transparent machine-settlement plus a richer template set than basic binary products. The core risk is strategic diffusion: too many segments, too many templates, and too many narratives before one wedge is proven.

## 2) One-line strategy

**Nail one high-frequency deterministic wedge, prove repeat behavior, then expand into advanced and mainstream utility markets with strict gating.**

## 3) Hard reality check (from roast analysis)

Biggest weaknesses today:
- Strategy identity is still too broad (crypto loop + mainstream utility + creator economy all at once).
- Capability-story mismatch can overpromise what is safely launchable now.
- Trust advantage is not yet fully productized in user-facing UX.
- Innovation breadth risks liquidity fragmentation and PMF dilution.

Bottom line: execution discipline is the bottleneck, not idea generation.

## 4) Market landscape in one page

Direct competition is strong in narrow slices:
- high-frequency binary loop products are crowded,
- broad prediction platforms have stronger brand,
- regulated event venues have credibility but slower product cycles.

RetroPick’s viable position:
- deterministic-first product trust,
- template depth beyond plain up/down,
- creator-led distribution built on clear market rules.

## 5) TAM / SAM / SOM (planning ranges)

Top-down annual gross participation potential:
- TAM: **$8B - $45B** (base **$20B**)
- SAM: **$600M - $4.5B** (base **$1.8B**)
- SOM (12-24 months): **$6M - $70M** (base **$22M**)

Bottom-up base case anchor:
- year-end MAU: 35k,
- active weeks per user-year: 18,
- markets per active week: 3.2,
- avg stake: $11,
- annual volume: ~**$22.2M**,
- effective take: 2.8%,
- fee revenue proxy: ~**$622k**.

Interpretation:
- TAM is story.
- SAM is design.
- SOM is execution.
- Only SOM should drive near-term operating decisions.

## 6) PMF decision framework

PMF means repeat behavior and trust, not temporary traffic.

Primary PMF gates:
- W4 retention threshold hit in at least two segments,
- stable low invalid/void incident profile,
- creator-sourced acquisition with healthy cohort quality,
- 8+ weeks of positive volume and repeat trend.

If gates are not met, do not broaden templates; narrow and iterate.

## 7) Innovation policy

Innovation tracks are valid, but sequencing is everything:
- **Now:** direction/threshold/range (+ selective velocity).
- **Next:** ladder/convergence/composite if baseline PMF is stable.
- **Later:** path-dependent and conditional systems after operational trust and liquidity concentration are proven.

Innovation should be approved only if it increases repeat usage without harming liquidity concentration.

## 8) 90-day CEO priorities (exact)

1. **Lock launch identity (Week 1).**  
   Publish one canonical narrative and stop mixed positioning.

2. **Enforce launch-safe template portfolio (Week 1-2).**  
   Hard-cap active templates and define kill criteria.

3. **Ship trust UX as a first-class feature (Week 2-5).**  
   Every market must expose lock, close, source, formula, and outcome proof clearly.

4. **Run PMF sprint cadence (Weeks 2-12).**  
   One falsifiable hypothesis every 2 weeks with explicit thresholds.

5. **Operational reliability governance (Weeks 1-12).**  
   Weekly incident review, oracle/lifecycle reliability dashboard, and no feature expansion without reliability gate pass.

6. **Creator distribution pilot with quality guardrails (Weeks 4-12).**  
   Track creator-sourced retention, not vanity referral volume.

7. **Board-level KPI reporting cadence (starting Week 4).**  
   Weekly: retention, repeat participation, concentration ratio, invalid rate, creator cohort quality.

## 9) 90-day no-regret metrics

- W4 retention by segment,
- markets per active user per week,
- concentration ratio of top templates,
- invalid/void rate,
- creator-sourced user share and quality,
- gross participation volume and effective take.

If these do not improve together, strategy is not working regardless of top-line acquisition.

## 10) Board recommendation

Approve a focused 90-day execution plan with strict gating:
- no broad template expansion before PMF evidence,
- no mainstream push before onboarding/trust UX proof,
- no narrative drift from deterministic wedge positioning.

RetroPick’s win condition is not more features; it is compounding trust and repeat behavior in a narrow wedge, then scaling deliberately.
