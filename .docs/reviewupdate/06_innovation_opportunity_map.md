# RetroPick Innovation Opportunity Map

## 1) Objective

Expand current idea seeds into an actionable innovation map grounded in:
- existing smart contract capabilities,
- realistic near-term product execution,
- PMF-oriented demand from both crypto and non-crypto users worldwide.

## 2) Idea seed expansion from `.agent/validat-idea.md`

Original seeds:
1. exotic multi-type prediction markets,
2. personal futures reputation markets,
3. scientific replication markets,
4. conditional nested markets,
5. multi-leg conditional discovery feed.

Expanded into prioritized tracks below.

## 3) Opportunity ranking framework

Each opportunity is scored on:
- **Feasibility now** (contract + ops readiness),
- **PMF speed** (time to measurable repeat behavior),
- **Differentiation** (defensible product edge),
- **Expansion potential** (cross-segment growth).

## 5) Crypto user needs and mapped products

Global crypto users need:
- fast recurring expression loops,
- richer structures than binary rounds,
- transparent settlement and low trust overhead,
- social/creator context for market discovery.

Mapped products:
- Pulse templates (`Direction`, `Threshold`, `RangeClose`) for recurring activity,
- velocity and ladder style products for advanced users,
- creator campaign pages with transparent market mechanics.

## 6) Non-crypto user needs and mapped products

Global non-crypto users need:
- plain language outcomes,
- understandable timing and settlement rules,
- utility framing (risk expression/hedging), not pure speculation language,
- confidence that outcomes are objective and auditable.

Mapped products:
- threshold/range products linked to highly recognized indicators/assets,
- daily/weekly cadence rather than high-frequency overload,
- explicit post-market proof cards (lock, close, formula, winner).

## 7) Opportunity tracks by horizon

## Now (0-6 months)
- Deterministic recurring crypto templates with concentrated liquidity.
- Basic creator distribution mechanics for referral and thesis sharing.
- Utility-oriented narrative tests on threshold/range formats.

## Next (6-12 months)
- Composite and convergence market families for advanced signal products.
- Region/category-specific deterministic market bundles.
- Deeper creator analytics and reputation surfaces.

## Later (12-24 months)
- Path-dependent and conditional multi-leg systems.
- Personal futures adjacent products (if legal/compliance conditions support).
- Scientific/enterprise forecasting vertical pilots.

## 8) Innovation thesis by segment

## Crypto-native segment
Innovation is not "more markets." It is better expressive structures with retained simplicity.

## Mainstream/non-crypto segment
Innovation is trust + clarity + relevance, not complexity.

## Creator segment
Innovation is programmable, objective market creation with distribution economics.

## 9) Non-negotiable innovation filters

Every new market concept must pass:
1. deterministic settlement feasibility,
2. clear user explanation in one market card,
3. measurable repeat behavior hypothesis,
4. operational reliability within existing lifecycle constraints,
5. legal/compliance viability for target region.

## 10) Recommended innovation roadmap

1. **Exploit now-capable deterministic templates first.**
2. **Use creator-led growth to expand distribution before adding deep complexity.**
3. **Introduce advanced templates only when baseline PMF metrics stabilize.**
4. **Approach high-novelty ideas (personal futures, scientific replication, conditional stacks) as controlled pilots, not core launch strategy.**
